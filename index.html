01 / THE HEART

LIFE IS
LOUD.

We live surrounded by noise.

Schedules. Responsibilities. Notifications. Expectations.

And sometimes, what we need isn't another event to attend.

We need room.

Room to breathe.
Room to listen.
Room to pray.
Room to worship.
Room to become aware of God again.

That's the heart behind MAKOM.

A place to step away from the noise and make room for God's presence.

02 / THE GATHERING

COME.
BE STILL.

MAKOM is built around three simple rhythms , creating room to turn our attention toward God and respond to His presence.

01

WORSHIP

Unhurried, Jesus-centred worship. Not a performance. A space to turn our attention toward Him and respond.

02

PRAYER

Space to seek God personally and together, bring what you're carrying before Him, and receive prayer.

03

STILLNESS

Moments to stop, listen, reflect, and become aware of God's presence. Not every moment needs to be filled with words.

03 / COME AS YOU ARE

THERE IS
ROOM
FOR YOU.

You don't have to have everything figured out.

Maybe you've followed Jesus for years. Maybe you're tired. Maybe you're searching. Maybe you're simply longing for a quiet place to meet with God.

MAKOM is a place to come as you are , to slow down, breathe, worship, pray, listen, and make room for God's presence.

NO PERFORMANCE. NO PRESSURE. JUST COME.
